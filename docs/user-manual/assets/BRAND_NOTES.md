# Northstar Flow demonstration brand

Northstar Flow is a fictional premium reusable-bottle brand created solely for the Marketing Media System user manual. It is safe to use in screenshots, examples and generated draft media.

- Product: 750 ml insulated stainless-steel bottle
- Audience: active urban professionals aged 25-45
- Proposition: reliable cold water from the commute to the trail
- Message: "Cold all day. Ready anywhere."
- Call to action: "Choose your route at northstarflow.example"
- Visual direction: modern British outdoor editorial; city-to-hill transitions; crisp dawn light; tactile details
- Voice: capable, calm, concise and encouraging
- Approved claims: "Keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours" and "Made with recycled stainless steel"
- Prohibited claims: health outcomes, carbon-neutral claims and "100% sustainable"
- Required disclaimer: "Performance varies with use and conditions."
